Terms of use

Use Velaur Media Studio to process media you are permitted to use. Source services may restrict access; availability is not guaranteed.

Trial and plans

The introductory Creator trial starts when you choose Unlock free trial. The default trial lasts 30 days and includes 10 hours of successfully exported video/audio, plus daily limits. Your dashboard displays effective access and usage. Cached previews may be reused without a new job.

Creator, Pro and Studio pricing is displayed for future paid access. Checkout is disabled, no payment card is collected, and trials do not automatically become paid subscriptions.

Processing and files

Source duration, file size, quality and processing slots depend on your access limits and server capacity. Source restrictions or processing failures can prevent completion. Completed exports are available for up to 24 hours. Keep your own copies of important media.

Account access

Accounts may be paused or blocked for service protection or misuse. Administrators can adjust access and limits; the dashboard reflects current settings. Contact VELAUR LABS through velaurlabs.com for assistance.
