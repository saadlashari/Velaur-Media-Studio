Trim video or extract audio online

Velaur Media Studio is a browser-based video tool by VELAUR LABS. Previews and exports are prepared on our server. Sign in with Google, check your access, then open the Studio.

How do I trim a video?

Upload a video or paste a supported public link. Click Preview, set the start and end timestamps, choose MP4, then click Export media. Follow the processing status and save the file when it is ready.

How do I extract MP3 audio?

Select MP3 instead of MP4. Keep the full video or choose a clip, then export. The source needs an audio track. MP3 conversion creates an audio file; it does not improve the source recording.

Why can a video link fail?

Private, removed or region-restricted videos may be unavailable. A source service can also refuse access. Upload an authorized local copy as an alternative. Processing time depends on the source, clip length and server capacity.

Media Studio: common questions answered

Can I trim a video without installing software?

Yes. Velaur Media Studio offers browser-based clip selection. Media processing runs on the service's server and requires eligible account access.

Does Media Studio work with every YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook video?

No. Availability depends on the source permissions, platform access rules and regional restrictions. Only process media you have permission to use. Authorized local files can be uploaded when a link is unavailable.

What is the difference between MP4 and MP3 export?

MP4 retains video and audio. MP3 is audio-only output, so the video picture is not included.

Can I buy a paid plan online today?

Paid checkout is not currently available. Review the Creator, Pro and Studio pricing and allowances for current trial and access information.

A FEW GOOD ANSWERS

Before you hit export.

A little more about video trimming, supported files and audio conversion.

Choose MP4 for video or MP3 for audio. Preview first to select a clip, then export and follow the processing status.
Can I convert a video to MP3?

Yes. Choose MP3 as the output format, select an audio bitrate, and export the full video or a selected clip. The source must contain an audio track.

Which video files can I upload?

You can upload MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI and M4V files. Playback in the preview player depends on your browser and the file's codecs.

Can I trim a video without installing an editor?

Yes. Add a video, preview it, choose your start and end timestamps, and export your clip. Local files are uploaded for export processing.

Why do some video links fail?

A video may be private, removed, restricted in a region or unavailable to the source service. Check the error shown by the tool, try another public link, or upload a video file you can access.

Does a higher resolution improve the original video?

No. The resolution setting limits the output height. It does not restore detail missing from the source. Final quality depends on the original video and your export settings.
