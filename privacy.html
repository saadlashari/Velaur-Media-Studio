Privacy

Velaur Media Studio is operated by VELAUR LABS.

Google sign-in

We receive your Google account identifier, name and verified email to create your account. We do not receive or store your Google password. Sessions use secure cookies; account records include signup and last-login timestamps.

Media and usage

Video links and uploaded files are processed on our server to prepare previews and exports. Public TikTok links may be sent to TikWM to resolve media. Source-service account cookies are not sent to that resolver.

We record job ownership, export duration, usage and account access. Export downloads expire after 24 hours; temporary files are removed by server cleanup. Account and audit records are retained to operate the service and investigate access issues.

Administration

Authorized administrators can review accounts, manage access and inspect administrative audit history. Media jobs are not publicly listed. Billing is disabled; we do not collect payment card details.

Free trial protection

To prevent repeated free trials, we keep a secure browser cookie and keyed hashes of the network address and browser identifier, linked to the account that activated the trial. A network or browser can activate one introductory trial. People sharing a network may need assistance from support. These records do not replace Google sign-in and do not restrict manually granted access. The cookie lasts up to one year; trial claim records are retained for abuse prevention. Contact us for account or data-removal requests.

Requests

For account or data-removal requests, contact VELAUR LABS through velaurlabs.com. You can also manage Google authorization through your Google account settings.
